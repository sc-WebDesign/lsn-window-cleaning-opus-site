## Two, and why there are not more

This trade nearly defeated the programme. An earlier iteration recorded window cleaning as a
**zero-stock trade** after roughly nineteen rejected candidates across three separate window-cleaning
builds, and left an instruction not to bother looking again.

**That verdict was reached with an older search method and was re-tested once here**, with the
current one, which found usable frames on the first attempt. So the trade is **thin and hazardous
rather than empty**. Two good photographs is what it yields, and two is what is here.

## The constraint that removes most of this trade's stock

The business report allows **no claim about ladders, poles, heights, gutters, conservatories or
pressure washing**, because none of them appears anywhere in the evidence.

**A photograph claims as loudly as a sentence does.** That rules out almost everything window
cleaning stock actually contains: skyscraper work, harnesses, glass roofs, water-fed poles and
cherry pickers. What survives is ground-level squeegee and cloth work, which is what both of these
are, and which is also what a domestic round genuinely looks like.

## What was NOT removed, and why

This build carries two drawn elements, and **both stayed**. Neither is an illustration standing in
for a photograph:

- **The reminder text** is a mock message, and the fact that he texts before arriving is one of the
  few things his customers volunteer in reviews. It is the finding itself, and the page labels the
  wording illustrative twice.
- **The round calendar** is a schedule diagram, labelled illustrative, saying the real day is agreed
  on joining.

Judge a drawn element by what it substitutes for. These substitute for an explanation, not for a
camera.
