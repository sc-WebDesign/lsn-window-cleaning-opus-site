Questions, answered

FAQs

Do I need to be at home?+

No. Most customers are out. You will have had a text the day before, so all he usually needs is access to the back if there is a gate.

How often does he come?+

You choose the frequency when you join, commonly every four to eight weeks, and then it stays on the same rhythm without you having to book each time.

Are frames and sills included?+

Yes, they are part of the clean rather than an extra. It is the main reason people describe the job as thorough.

What happens if it rains?+

Windows cleaned properly do not mind a shower afterwards. If the weather makes the job impossible on the day, he will tell you and pick it up rather than skipping you.

How do I pay?+

Agreed when you join the round. Ring and ask what suits you both; it is a short conversation.
